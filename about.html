Our Story

Built on a Love for Clean Water

Summer Splash was founded in 2010 by Carlos Rivera, a lifelong pool enthusiast with a vision to bring professional, reliable pool services to Florida communities. What started as a one-person operation has grown into a trusted team of certified pool professionals serving hundreds of clients.

Today, we manage, maintain, renovate, and repair pools for commercial facilities and residential homeowners across the state. Our commitment to quality, transparency, and customer satisfaction remains at the heart of everything we do.
