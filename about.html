About Summer Splash

We're passionate about pools and dedicated to providing exceptional service to every client we serve.

More Than a Quick Skim

Summer Splash Pool Services was started because pools need more than a quick skim — they need a team with a pack mentality. We understand what a pool means to your property: it's resident happiness, lease renewals, and zero liability.

That's why we lead with wisdom to prevent problems before they happen, we keep pushing forward through the heat, the storms, and the tough water, and we operate with resilience to make sure your pool is clear, safe, and swim-ready every single day. We don't just service your pool, we become part of your property's pack.

Founded in 2020 by Jodaine Miller, we serve Richmond, Hampton Roads and Southern Virginia — and at Summer Splash Pool Services, we treat everyone as family.
